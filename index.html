The short version

Third-year student, two internships in, happiest when a messy dataset finally makes sense.

I'm Kesavan G, studying Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at Ramco Institute of Technology, Rajapalayam. Most of my time goes into the unglamorous half of the job — cleaning data, questioning a feature that looks too good, rerunning a model until the validation stops lying to me.

The other half comes from drawing. Years of sketching taught me to look at a thing until the pattern shows up, and to explain it without a wall of text. That's why my dashboards tend to be readable and my slides tend to be short.

Right now I'm looking for an internship or a first role where I can own a model end to end — data in, decision out — and learn from people who've shipped more than I have.

Hybrid models beat clever single models

On smart-home load forecasting, an LSTM alone kept missing the spiky evenings. Pairing it with XGBoost and weighting each by inverse error got me R² 0.9528 and RMSE 0.1021, with 95% confidence bands on top.

2025 · energy forecasting

Edge hardware changes how you design

Four days on a Jetson Nano and a Raspberry Pi 3 — Linux, drivers, thermal limits, a model that ran fine on my laptop and crawled on the board. You design differently once you've felt the ceiling.

2025 · embedded

My first Kaggle score was negative

−20 on the PhysioNet ECG digitisation comp. Most of the fix was preprocessing and an honest validation split, not a bigger model. Finished 17+ and inside the top 300 of 10,000+.
